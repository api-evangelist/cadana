Cadana is a white-label global payment and payroll infrastructure provider for talent platforms, staffing companies, and HR providers. Its APIs let partners onboard and pay employees and contractors in 100+ countries, run multi-currency payroll, provision embedded wallets and virtual accounts, calculate gross-to-net tax across 150+ jurisdictions, and automate statutory filing and government remittance. The platform is delivered as five REST APIs (Workforce Management, Embedded Payments, Embedded Consumer Wallets, Global Tax Engine, and Statutory Compliance) with bearer-token auth, Svix-signed webhooks, a sandbox, and an embeddable white-label UI and salary-calculator widget. Backed by 500 Global.

**Areas:** Company · Payroll · Payments · Global Payroll · Contractor Payments · Embedded Finance · Wallets · Tax · Compliance · Fintech · Money Transfer · White Label

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Cadana: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
