Nigeria's PAYE system is administered at the state level — most employees have their tax remitted to the State Internal Revenue Service (IRS) where they reside, not to FIRS. However, FIRS administers PAYE for federal government agencies, diplomatic missions, and most foreign companies operating in Nigeria. TaxPro Max
